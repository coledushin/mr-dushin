Europe, in the middle of the nineteenth century

Two Nations Waiting to Be Born

In 1840 there was no country called Germany and no country called Italy. German speakers lived in 39 separate states. Italians lived in eight, several of them ruled by foreigners. By 1871 both had become powerful united nations.

How did it happen? You are about to find out by making the decisions yourself, with help from the people who were really there: Cavour, Garibaldi, Bismarck and more.

I.ListenYour advisors appear on screen to explain what is going on.
II.DecideChoose what to do, then see what really happened. Watch the map change.
III.WriteAnswer a few questions in your notebook and turn it in at the end.

How Prestige works: you earn the most points for choices that really would have worked at the time. That is not always the same as the fairest or kindest choice. Be ready to think about that!
